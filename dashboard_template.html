Metric definitions, data quality and limitations
  • Order count includes every status. Sales include item prices only on delivered orders; AOV divides those sales by delivered orders with item data. BRL is Brazilian real, not INR.
  • Late rate = late orders / eligible delivered orders. Actual delivery calendar date must be after promised calendar date. Same-day arrival is on time.
  • Eligible deliveries require valid purchase, actual delivery and estimated delivery dates; actual/estimated dates cannot precede purchase. Delivery days use timestamp differences.
  • Multiple review records are averaged per order; each reviewed order then gets equal weight. Missing reviews are excluded from review means.
  • Seller ranking excludes multi-seller orders because delivery timestamps describe the entire order. Carrier and destination effects remain possible.
  • Category order counts are not additive across categories. Merchandise sales are additive. Minimum sample thresholds are practical screening choices, not statistical tests.
  • These are historical observations, not causal effects or current marketplace results. No refund, cost or commission data are used, so sales do not represent profit or Olist revenue.
